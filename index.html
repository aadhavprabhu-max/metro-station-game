Metro lines run independently in both directions. Choose a starting line, approach an open door and press E to board. Explore your carriage and read the onboard screens.

At Central, U1 / U2 use upper platforms 01 / 02. U3 / U4 use lower platforms 03 / 04. U9 uses platform 09 at −48 m, connected by stairs from U3; change to U5 at Nordklinikum and Waldtor. Leave your train and follow the passage and stair signs to change lines.
