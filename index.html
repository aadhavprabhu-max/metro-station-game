U1 connects Nordplatz, Central and Rosenheimer Platz. U2 connects Stadtzentrum, Central, Schwarzkopf-Tunnel and Eisenwerk. Both trains run continuously in both directions.

To change lines, leave your train at Central and follow the signed passage between U1 platform 01 and U2 platform 02. Approach an open door and press E to board.
